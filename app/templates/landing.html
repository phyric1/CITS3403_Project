How to Play

Dungeon Dealer is a turn-based, top-down dungeon crawler where your goal is to complete a series of dungeon floors while surviving enemies, collecting loot, and building a powerful card deck.

Core Gameplay
You move through dungeon floors filled with enemies, gold, keys, and locked doors using the arrow keys. To progress, you must find keys to unlock doors and reach the end of each floor. Completing the required number of floors wins the game.

Each turn, your deck is shuffled, and you draw cards that provide abilities, buffs, or stat boosts. These cards are used strategically during movement and combat by clicking on them.

Cards & Deck Building
You start with a basic deck, but can customise it over time by adding, removing, or trading cards. Some cards can be marked as tradable, allowing other players to offer exchanges.

Cards can be obtained through dungeon rewards, shops, or card packs purchased with tokens earned from completing runs.

Economy & Shops
During runs, you collect gold which can be spent in shops to buy daily cards. You can also earn difficulty-based tokens by completing runs, which are used to buy card packs.

Trading System
Players can search for cards and trade with others by sending offers that include multiple cards on both sides. Trade requests are sent as notifications and can be accepted or declined by the other player.

Progression & Competition
Track your performance through player statistics, graphs, and a global leaderboard. Compare wins, losses, and overall performance against other players.

Build stronger decks, improve your strategy, and climb the leaderboard to become the top dungeon dealer.
